Conceptual workflow · select a step to explore

  1. Valid claim → retrieve policies. Missing identifiers → human review.
  2. Continue to deterministic checks.
  3. Findings inform the second retrieval pass.
  4. Conditional: skip when there are no findings. Continue to AI.
  5. Continue to risk controls.
  6. Branch: automatic recommendation or human review.
  7. Rules and model agree, with no review triggers → record audit.
  8. Alternative branch: escalation with reasons → record audit.
  9. Both outcomes are recorded with evidence and execution metadata.
